Ribbon Particle Reverse Engineering

Catatan membongkar ribbon melengkung dari PNG yang lurus, di Minecraft Bedrock.Notes on taking apart a curved ribbon built from a straight PNG, in Minecraft Bedrock.

Bahan: Ribbon_Warden_Soul.zip, warden.json, f2da1072.json, animation_controllers.zip, particles (102 file berisi sealpe), entity.zip, animations.zip, rhs.json.Sources: Ribbon_Warden_Soul.zip, warden.json, f2da1072.json, animation_controllers.zip, particles (102 files containing sealpe), entity.zip, animations.zip, rhs.json.

Jawaban singkatShort answer

PNG-nya memang cuma strip lurus 20×240: kepala putih di atas, ekor biru yang makin transparan ke bawah. Lengkungannya tidak ada di gambar dan tidak ada di mesh. Ribbon adalah barisan segmen lurus pendek. Dua ujung tiap segmen dihitung rumus Molang (spiral meredam), dan tiap segmen adalah satu billboard yang diputar searah garis ekor ke kepala.The PNG really is just a straight 20×240 strip: white head on top, a blue tail that gets more transparent downward. The curve is not in the image and not in a mesh. A ribbon is a row of short straight segments. Both ends of each segment are computed by a Molang formula (a damped spiral), and each segment is one billboard rotated along the tail-to-head line.

Dua jalur yang ditemukanTwo paths we found

Fakta kunciKey facts

  • Anak wrbaad membaca 37 variabel dari luar. Induk endckw mengisi semuanya di creation_expression, termasuk 4 angka acak dari v.emitter_random_1 sampai _4.The child wrbaad reads 37 variables from outside. The parent endckw fills all of them in creation_expression, including 4 random numbers from v.emitter_random_1 to _4.
  • Anak hanya aktif bila v.sealpe benar (v.sealpe??0). Induk mengisinya lewat pre_effect_expression: v.sealpe=t.lot.The child is only active when v.sealpe is true (v.sealpe??0). The parent sets it through pre_effect_expression: v.sealpe=t.lot.
  • Anak punya emitter_rate_instant 1 dan creation_event yang mengisi v.wnaarx=1: emitter anak langsung expired, tapi partikelnya hidup sampai segmen habis.The child has emitter_rate_instant 1 and a creation_event that sets v.wnaarx=1: the child emitter expires immediately, but its particle lives until the segment is done.
  • Kepala dan ekor segmen: v.mbjzhu dan v.adphax (waktu kurva), posisinya v.kgobpa... dan v.sbhmli...; UV dipotong lewat v.nviqsz dan v.sbiadh.Head and tail of a segment: v.mbjzhu and v.adphax (curve time), positions v.kgobpa... and v.sbhmli...; the UV is sliced with v.nviqsz and v.sbiadh.
  • Partikel Bedrock bisa memutar suara lewat sound_effect di events.Bedrock particles can play sounds through sound_effect in events.

    Dari 1062 pemanggilan partikel, yang paling banyak mengisi variabel hanya 6. sealpe nol kemunculan di entity, animasi, dan controller.Out of 1062 particle calls, the most any of them sets is 6 variables. sealpe has zero occurrences in the entity, animations, and controllers.

  6. Batch search sealpe di folder particles.Batch search for sealpe in the particles folder.

    102 file. Di antaranya rhz.json (ribbon:endckw) yang memanggil ribbon:wrbaad lewat event.102 files. One of them is rhz.json (ribbon:endckw), which calls ribbon:wrbaad from an event.

  7. Induk endckw.The parent endckw.

    creation_expression mengisi umur acak Math.random(2.5,4), empat angka acak, dan konstanta fisika. Timeline 121 titik (30 Hz, 0 sampai 4 detik) memanggil event bsopxi: hitung segmen berikutnya (v.ufzvdc sebagai counter), lalu panggil anak dengan pre_effect_expression (v.sealpe=t.lot;...).creation_expression sets a random lifetime Math.random(2.5,4), four random numbers, and the physics constants. A 121-point timeline (30 Hz, 0 to 4 seconds) calls event bsopxi: it computes the next segment (v.ufzvdc as a counter), then calls the child with pre_effect_expression (v.sealpe=t.lot;...).

  8. Siapa yang memanggil endckw?Who calls endckw?

    Tidak ada di 128 entity, 17 animasi, 9 controller, maupun partikel lain. Ia tidak butuh input (hanya v.wthuxi??0 opsional), jadi cukup /particle ribbon:endckw ~ ~1 ~. Dites dengan command block repeat: muncul banyak ribbon melengkung kiri-kanan.Nobody does: not in 128 entities, 17 animation files, 9 controllers, or other particles. It needs no input (only an optional v.wthuxi??0), so /particle ribbon:endckw ~ ~1 ~ is enough. Tested with a repeating command block: many ribbons curving left and right appeared.

  9. pxabgv.pxabgv.

    Di rhs.json ia dispatcher: partikel ukuran 0 dan umur 0, expiration_event memanggil ribbon:hzxbiw dengan v.kozdua=4. hzxbiw mengaktifkan satu anak lewat v.sealpe=t.wyp==N; rantainya sampai ribbon:mdeyzy dan ribbon:kjnksh.In rhs.json it is a dispatcher: a particle of size 0 and lifetime 0 whose expiration_event calls ribbon:hzxbiw with v.kozdua=4. hzxbiw activates one child through v.sealpe=t.wyp==N; the chain goes on to ribbon:mdeyzy and ribbon:kjnksh.

    mdeyzy memainkan tripod dan wind_charge.burst; kjnksh memainkan sonic_boom, respawn_anchor.deplete, dan particle.soul_escape.loud.mdeyzy plays tripod and wind_charge.burst; kjnksh plays sonic_boom, respawn_anchor.deplete, and particle.soul_escape.loud.

Koreksi di tengah jalanCorrections along the way

Dugaan awal “ribbon = mesh” salah: hanya billboard.The first guess “ribbon = mesh” was wrong: it is only billboards.
pxabgv awalnya dikira warden_soul. Bukan: warden_soul = wrbaad, sedangkan pxabgv hanya dispatcher efek kematian.pxabgv was first thought to be warden_soul. It is not: warden_soul = wrbaad, while pxabgv is only the death effect dispatcher.
“Partikel tidak bisa bersuara” salah: bisa lewat sound_effect.“Particles cannot play sounds” was wrong: they can, through sound_effect.

Cara kerjaHow it works

  1. Emitter mengisi konstanta dan 4 angka acak (emitter_random_1 sampai _4). Semua partikel berbagi angka itu, jadi semua potongan berada di kurva yang sama.The emitter sets the constants and 4 random numbers (emitter_random_1 to _4). All particles share them, so all pieces lie on the same curve.
  2. emitter_rate_steady memunculkan 8 partikel per detik. Tiap partikel adalah satu ubin waktu kurva [j/8, (j+1)/8] dengan j = floor((emitter_age - particle_age) * rate + 0.2).emitter_rate_steady spawns 8 particles per second. Each particle is one curve-time tile [j/8, (j+1)/8] with j = floor((emitter_age - particle_age) * rate + 0.2).
  3. Kepala ribbon ada di waktu kurva T = emitter_age, ekor di T - L dengan L = len * (0.52 + 0.48 * T / life). Bagian ubin yang terlihat adalah irisan ubin dengan [T-L, T]: lo dan hi.The ribbon head sits at curve time T = emitter_age and the tail at T - L with L = len * (0.52 + 0.48 * T / life). The visible part of a tile is its overlap with [T-L, T]: lo and hi.
  4. Titik P(lo) dan P(hi) dihitung rumus spiral meredam (exp, cos, sin) yang sama dengan wrbaad (selisih numerik sekitar 1e-8). Kenapa tanpa menulis v.* dari partikelWhy no v.* writes from the particle

Tidak pasti apakah variabel yang ditulis di ekspresi partikel milik satu partikel atau dibagi satu emitter. Karena itu setiap komponen menghitung ulang nilainya dengan variabel lokal t.*, aman untuk dua kemungkinan itu.
